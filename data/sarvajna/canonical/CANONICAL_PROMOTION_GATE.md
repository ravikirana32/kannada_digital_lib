# Phase 6 — Canonical Promotion Gate

A discovered verse becomes canonical only after all required evidence is recorded:

1. Source identity and locator
2. Exact source text
3. Cross-source correspondence check
4. Variant registration
5. Authenticity status/qualification
6. Rights/provenance review
7. Editorial approval

A candidate may remain `unresolved` indefinitely. Missing evidence must never be filled by guesswork.

Canonical IDs are stable and independent of source numbering.
