# Human Review Checklist — SAR-0001 to SAR-0005

- [ ] Historical source identified
- [ ] Edition/year verified from title page or trusted catalogue

- [ ] Exact Kannada transcription checked

- [ ] Other source readings compared
- [ ] Variants recorded
- [ ] Authenticity status assessed
- [ ] Rights/provenance assessed
- [ ] Canonical wording selected
- [ ] Editorial approval recorded
- [ ] Bhavartha prepared from approved canonical wording
- [ ] Publication/social outputs generated
