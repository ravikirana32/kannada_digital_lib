# Phase 5B — Canonical Selection Rules

A verse can be selected as canonical only when:

1. The source correspondence is established.
2. At least one authoritative historical/critical reading is verified.
3. Material variants are recorded.
4. Authenticity is assessed or explicitly marked disputed.
5. Rights/provenance is documented.
6. Editorial review approves the exact Kannada text.

## Important

A high text-similarity score is not sufficient to establish identity.
A modern website is not automatically an authoritative edition.
A source number is never treated as a universal Sarvagna number.
