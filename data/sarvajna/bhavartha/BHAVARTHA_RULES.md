# Bhavartha Rules — Phase 5

1. Do not write bhavartha from an unverified web transcription.
2. Use only the approved canonical Kannada text as the source.
3. Preserve the meaning; do not add unsupported historical claims.
4. Keep the explanation in natural Kannada.
5. Clearly separate literal meaning from interpretive explanation.
6. Bhavartha status remains blocked until canonical text approval.
