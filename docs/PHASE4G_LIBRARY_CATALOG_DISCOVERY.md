# Phase 4G — Library/Catalog Discovery

## Result

Today's search strengthened the bibliography but did **not** locate an exact, inspectable scan of the 1924 edition.

The best evidence confirms:
- the 1924 edition existed,
- Channappa Uttangi edited it,
- the work was the result of long manuscript/printed-source research,
- later editions followed,
- the 1924 version is reported as 1,928 poems.

## New discovery leads

1. Mysore University Encyclopedia — strongest bibliographic evidence.
2. Shastriya Kannada — specialist scholarly biography and edition history.
3. Kannada Sahitya Parishattu — institutional biography.
4. University of Madras — academic reading-list evidence.
5. Current Uttangi-edited edition listing — corroborating the 1,928 count.

None of these should be mistaken for the actual 1924 scan.

## Why we stop before declaring SRC-003 verified

A source title/year in a catalogue is not enough to reconstruct the exact text. We need a scan or a trusted page-level bibliographic record showing the 1924 edition's title page/colophon.

## Important research finding

The current evidence also says the first modern book collection was around 300 poems in 1868, followed by Uttangi's 1924 collection; later editions grew in number. This reinforces the need for edition-aware reconciliation rather than a single global count.
