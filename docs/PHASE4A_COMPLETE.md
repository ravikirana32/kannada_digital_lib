## Current source strategy

**SRC-001**: Archive.org / Uttangi 1957 — historical reference.

**SRC-002**: Kannada Wikisource — machine-readable/reference candidate; underlying source and rights still require verification.

The University of Mysore encyclopedia describes Uttangi’s 1924 *Sarvajna Vachanagalu* as a major edited work and says he spent about nine years on it and edited around 2,000 vachanas. The final corpus must therefore be established through documented source comparison rather than assuming a fixed count.
