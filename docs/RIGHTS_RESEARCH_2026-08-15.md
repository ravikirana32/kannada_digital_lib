# Rights Research — 2026-08-15

## India

Section 22 of the Indian Copyright Act states that copyright in a published literary work generally lasts until 60 years from the beginning of the calendar year following the author's death.

Scholarly sources identify Channappa Uttangi as 1881–1962 and identify his 1924 *Sarvajna Vachanagalu* as an edited work. On that basis, the editor's ordinary literary copyright term would have ended after the 1962 term.

## Important qualification

The Mysore University encyclopedia states that the Mysore Government acquired ownership of the work from Uttangi and subsequently published editions. That creates a rights-chain question that must be resolved before the repository labels the 1924 edition unconditionally public domain.

Therefore SRC-003 remains:

`candidate_public_domain_india_needs_legal_verification`

This project must not present this as legal advice or as a universal worldwide public-domain determination.

## Practical publication policy

Until the rights chain is documented:
- use SRC-003 as a research/canonical candidate,
- do not claim "copyright free worldwide",
- retain source attribution,
- preserve edition metadata,
- and if commercial/worldwide publication is planned, obtain a legal review.
