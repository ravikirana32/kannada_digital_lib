# Phase 4F — 1924 Uttangi Source Discovery

## Today's research result

We confirmed strong bibliographic evidence for Uttangi's 1924 *Sarvajna Vachanagalu*, but **did not locate a directly downloadable/inspectable scan of the exact 1924 edition** in today's search.

### Confirmed

- University of Mysore Encyclopedia: *Sarvajna Vachanagalu (1924)* was edited by Channappa Uttangi; the editing work ran from 1915–1924; the work was later acquired by the Mysore Government and appeared in multiple editions.
- Specialist Kannada scholarship: the 1924 version contained 1,928 poems and was organized into three broad categories and 32 thematic subdivisions.
- Kannada Wikisource provides a large machine-readable Sarvajna corpus, but it is not automatically the same edition as the 1924 book.
- Internet Archive's 1957 item is a later edition and its metadata says IN_COPYRIGHT.

## Search result

The following were checked during today's discovery:
- Archive/Internet Archive search results for a 1924 Uttangi edition
- Open Library/catalog-style results
- Kannada web results for the exact title/year/editor combination
- Kannada Wikisource bibliographic material

No result found today provides a verified scan that can safely be labeled **"1924 Uttangi edition"**.

## Critical rule

Do **not** use a 1935, 1941, 1957, 1980, or modern edition as SRC-003 merely because it is easier to obtain.

The edition year and editor must be verified from the title page/colophon or a trusted bibliographic record.
