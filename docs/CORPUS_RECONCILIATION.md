# Phase 4D — Independent Source Discovery & Corpus Reconciliation

## Goal

Determine the complete Sarvajna corpus without assuming that one website's numbering is complete or canonical.

### Current evidence

- Kannada Wikisource contains a large numbered collection and thematic sections.
- Kannada scholarly reference material says Uttangi's 1924 edited edition contained about 2,000 vachanas.
- A specialist scholarly source gives the 1924 Uttangi version as **1,928 poems** and says the collection was classified into spiritual, ethical and materialistic categories and 32 thematic subdivisions.
- The Archive.org 1957 scan is a useful historical reference but its metadata marks the item `IN_COPYRIGHT`.

### New candidate source

`SRC-003` = Uttangi's 1924 edition.

This is **not yet marked public domain**. Indian Copyright Office materials state that published literary works generally have a term of the author's life plus 60 years. Since scholarly sources identify Uttangi's death as 1962, the 1924 edition appears to be a strong public-domain-in-India candidate, but the project must verify the chain of title/assignment and the jurisdictions in which we intend to publish.

### Reconciliation model

For every candidate verse:

1. source-specific number
2. source locator
3. normalized text fingerprint
4. exact/near match candidates
5. variant class
6. canonical decision
7. rights basis
8. editorial approval

### Important

Do not collapse different source numbers into a single global number until the correspondence is documented.

Do not copy a modern copyrighted commentary or edited explanation into the canonical corpus.

## Current target

Reconcile the first 5 pilot records across SRC-001, SRC-002 and SRC-003 before scaling.
