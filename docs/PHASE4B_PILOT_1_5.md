# Phase 4B — Pilot 1–5

## Status

Five research records have now been created from the current Kannada Wikisource text.

### Important editorial finding

The first five are in the **ಜಾತಿಸ್ಮರಣ ಪದ್ದತಿ** section and concern traditional/biographical claims about Sarvajna's origin. They are therefore not ideal examples of general ethical "wisdom" verses.

Scholarly material on Channappa Uttangi notes that many Sarvajna poems had variants and interpolations. The project therefore keeps these five in `editorial_review` and does not mark them `approved`.
